Use the individual agreement below when you own the contribution rights, or the
corporate agreement when the employer owns them. An individual cannot grant rights
owned by their employer. In that case, the employer signs the corporate agreement
and supplies the contributor list.

## 0. When this agreement is used

Ordinary contributions are accepted with a DCO sign-off: they come in under
Apache-2.0 Section 5 and nothing more is asked. For a substantial contribution
(the criteria are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) §1) the Company asks for
this agreement before merging, because a DCO attests provenance but does not
contain the sublicensing and patent grants below. The agreement adds a
requirement for those contributions; it is not represented as a benefit.

This is a **license, not an assignment**. Contributors retain copyright.

## B. Corporate Contributor License Agreement

The provisions of A apply, supplemented rather than replaced by the following.

### B.1 Authorized signatory

The signatory must have authority to enter this agreement for the legal entity.
An unauthorized signature does not establish the required contribution rights.

### B.2 Covered contributors

This agreement covers Contributions from employees listed in B.4. The entity must
maintain and submit updates to that list when employees are added or leave. An
unlisted employee's Contribution is not covered by this agreement.

### B.3 Departing employees

Removing an employee from the list does not terminate licenses covering Contributions
submitted while that employee was listed.
